Cookies & Tracking Policy

How we use cookies to provide the best deal experience

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your preferences (such as copied promo codes or category filters) and provide a smooth browsing experience.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

🔒 Essential & Functional Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These are necessary for the website to operate correctly, remember your cookie consent preferences, and ensure modal windows function properly.

📊 Performance & Analytics Cookies

Help us understand which coupons are most popular, which stores get visited, and how to improve site speed and interface performance.

🛍️ Affiliate & Referral Cookies

When you click "Get Code" or "Get Deal", a referral cookie may be set by the affiliate merchant network so the store recognizes you came from HerPromoCodes. This does not track personal identifying information.

3. Managing Your Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings or directly via our Cookie Preferences popup on this page.
