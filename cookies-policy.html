Cookies Policy

Learn how cookies improve your coupon search experience

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of text stored on your device by your web browser when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you logged in where applicable, and understand how visitors interact with site features.

2. How PromoBella Uses Cookies

PromoBella uses cookies to deliver a fast, seamless experience and verify working discount codes. Specifically:

  • Essential & Functional Cookies: Necessary for basic website functions, navigation between store pages, search filtering, and mobile menu toggles.
  • Analytics & Performance Cookies: Help us measure which store coupons are clicked most frequently and identify technical issues so we can improve site speed.
  • Affiliate Tracking Cookies: When you click "Show Code" or "Get Deal", merchant affiliate networks set temporary referral cookies so the merchant knows you came from PromoBella. These cookies do not store private identifying information.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can manage, block, or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Most modern browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies and delete them individually.
  • Block third-party cookies or block all cookies completely.
  • Clear all cookies when you close your web browser.

Please note that if you choose to disable all cookies, some interactive features (such as saved filters or store redirects) may not function as intended.

4. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy periodically to reflect changes in our technologies or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated date.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our use of cookies, please contact us at support@promobella.com.
