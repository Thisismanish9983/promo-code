Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PromoBella.com. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and how we protect your personal data when you browse our discount coupons and shopping offers.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration to view or use coupons on PromoBella. The information we may collect includes:

  • Email Subscription: If you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter, we store your email address solely to deliver requested deal alerts and promotional updates.
  • Log Files & Usage Data: Standard server logs, browser type, referring pages, and device information to monitor website speed and stability.
  • Cookies: Small text files to remember your site preferences and verify affiliate coupon activations.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for legitimate business operations:

  • To present relevant, up-to-date promo codes and remove expired offers.
  • To send promotional email notifications (only if you subscribed).
  • To optimize the performance, security, and mobile layout of PromoBella.

4. Affiliate Links & Third-Party Merchants

PromoBella partners with merchant stores and affiliate marketing networks. When you click on a store offer, "Show Code", or "Get Deal" button, you may be redirected to the merchant's external website via an affiliate tracking link. If you complete a purchase, PromoBella may earn a referral commission at no additional cost to you.

Please note that external merchant sites have their own independent privacy policies, and PromoBella has no control over or liability for their practices.

5. Data Security

We implement standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption protocols, to protect submitted information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal email addresses to third parties.

6. Your Rights & Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe from our email notifications at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any email. For inquiries concerning your personal data, you may contact us at support@promobella.com.
